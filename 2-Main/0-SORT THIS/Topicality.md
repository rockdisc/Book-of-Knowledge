#Speech/Debate 

Topicality is a [[Negative]] strategy used to say that the [[Affirmative|Aff]]'s plan is not related to the [[Resolution]]. Here are the parts:

# Neg
## Interp 
This is the interpretation of the word. This is most often a [[Card]]. that says a definition that does not include the plan of the Aff. These can be as old as you want, though having it be too old can lead to arguments of the word not being used like that anymore. 

## Violation 
What the Aff violated in the ruling, like "they do not increase development by 10%". It is most often very small. 

## Standards 
This why the Neg's Interp is better, most common of the reasons are:
- Limits: It makes sure that there are not too many cases, allowing for prep for the neg to be possible. 
- Ground: It gives both sides a fair argument 
- Predictability: Plans should be able to be guessed from the resolution 
- Bright-line: Clear boundaries mean there is no need for judge intervention 
- Precision: having a precise topic allows for better debate
- Education: Having this interp allows for more education in the debate round
