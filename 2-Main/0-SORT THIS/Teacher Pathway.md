#Worldbuilding/Magic 

This is a fanmade(me) pathway using the [[Power System]] from [[Lord of Mysteries]]. 
# Symbolism 
Shapelessness, Knowledge Transfer, Creation/Materialization, Cognitovirus, Infohazard, 

## Sequence 9: Teacher
New abilities:
- **Orating/Presenting**. Allows for a **Teacher** to get every word that they want to get out, out. By speaking, the listener will want to hear every word that is being said and will understand even the most confusing concepts.
	- A **Teacher** can inherently find the best way to explain the topic to their student by somewhat accessing their spirit/knowledge body to get a better understanding of the person. 
- **Learning**. It can work on others and oneself. Through enhanced pattern recognition and better knowledge accumulation, a **Teacher** is able to identify new concepts or create connections leading to new discoveries or better hypotheses. 
	- They have much better memory, though not perfect, they can remember all of the information in a lecture and how it was presented. They can also remember how the students were feeling 
	- Their pattern recognition is better and they have an easier time not been tricked by false results in pattern recognition 

## Sequence 8: Writer

Strengthened Abilities:

- **Orating** can be used in written form and it more convincing 
- Writing will give major benefits to **Learning** allowing it to be much faster, even if they write in their **Working Memory**.
